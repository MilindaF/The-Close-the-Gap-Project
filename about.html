About Close the Gap

Building stronger connections between communities and the care, resources, and opportunities meant to serve them.

01

Vision

We envision healthier, more connected communities where people can navigate care with confidence, trusted resources reach the people they are meant to serve, and organizations work together so access to health and opportunity is not determined by circumstance.

02

Mission

Listen locally. Connect intentionally. Build with communities.

Close the Gap brings residents, community organizations, healthcare partners, educators, and funders together to identify barriers to care, strengthen access to trusted resources, and develop community-informed health initiatives.

03

Goals

Listen & Learn

Understand the barriers, priorities, and strengths communities identify for themselves.

Connect & Strengthen

Make trusted resources easier to find and strengthen connections among organizations already doing good work.

Develop & Educate

Create community-informed programming and prepare young people to become future health leaders.

Measure & Share

Evaluate what works, communicate impact clearly, and report back to communities and partners.

Our Approach

Community-informed by design.

Close the Gap does not begin with a predetermined solution. We begin with questions.

We listen to residents and organizations closest to the work, identify barriers and existing strengths, and use those insights to guide partnerships and programming. This approach helps reduce duplication, strengthen what already works, and keep community priorities at the center of growth.
