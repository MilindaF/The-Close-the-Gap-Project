Community

Local concerns should guide local programs.

We begin by learning from the people and organizations already closest to community needs—before deciding what new programs should exist.

Our Approach

We are starting by listening.

Close the Gap is meeting with community organizations, healthcare partners, and local leaders across South Florida to understand where people experience barriers before, during, and after seeking care.

These conversations help us identify what resources already exist, what may be difficult to find or use, and where additional support could add value without duplicating work that is already effective.

What we learn will guide future Community Conversations, partnerships, referral pathways, and community-informed programming.

Community Process

Listen first. Build second.

Our goal is to make sure new work responds to real barriers and strengthens what communities already trust.

01

Listen to community organizations

Document the concerns, barriers, and service gaps they are seeing locally.

02

Hold Community Conversations

Hear directly from residents about their experiences finding, understanding, and using care.

03

Map barriers and resources

Identify recurring problems alongside trusted services and assets that already exist.

04

Set priorities together

Use what we learn to guide practical partnerships, programs, referrals, and education.

05

Report back

Share what we learned, what we will pursue, and what we will not duplicate.

What We Are Exploring

Community-informed areas of action.

These are working areas—not predetermined programs. Community input will determine which ideas move forward and how they are designed.

01

Health Navigation

Helping people identify and understand trusted health and social resources.

02

Community Conversations

Creating structured spaces for residents to identify barriers, priorities, and local strengths.

03

Health Education & Connection

Connecting practical health information with organizations and services people can actually use.

Close the Gap NextGen

Preparing young people to understand—and help shape—community health.

NextGen gives students exposure to healthcare and public health careers while teaching them to think about health through the lens of access, community conditions, leadership, and equity.

NextGen Inquiry

Program experience

Students learn from healthcare professionals and community leaders, build practical leadership and communication skills, and complete a project focused on a community health issue.
