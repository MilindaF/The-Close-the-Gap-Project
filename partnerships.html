Partnerships

Better community health takes more than one organization.

Close the Gap is building relationships across community, healthcare, education, and philanthropy to strengthen access, avoid duplication, and create more connected pathways to support.

Who We Work With

Different partners. Shared purpose.

Partnerships may begin with a listening meeting, a referral relationship, a student opportunity, an in-kind contribution, or a larger collaborative program.

01

Community Partners

Share local insight, identify trusted resources, reach residents, host conversations, and help shape programming.

02

Healthcare Partners

Support education, navigation, referral pathways, speakers, career exposure, and community engagement.

03

Education Partners

Connect students to NextGen, mentors, leadership opportunities, and community health projects.

04

Funding Partners

Provide sponsorship, grants, space, technology, professional expertise, or in-kind support.

What Partnership Can Look Like

Start where there is shared value.

01

Listening & Community Insight

Participate in stakeholder listening meetings or help convene community voices.

02

Resource & Referral Connections

Help make trusted services easier to identify, understand, and reach.

03

Student Opportunity

Provide speakers, mentors, career exposure, site visits, or project guidance for NextGen.

04

Program Collaboration

Co-design or support community-informed health education and engagement efforts.
