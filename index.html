WHY CLOSE THE GAP

People may live near healthcare services and still struggle to find, understand, trust, afford, or use them. We focus on the barriers that exist between people and care.

Current Work

We are starting by listening.

Before building new programs, we are meeting with organizations and leaders who already serve South Florida communities.

These conversations help us understand the health concerns and access barriers they are seeing, which resources residents already use, where gaps remain, and where new efforts could add value without duplicating good work.

What we learn will shape future Community Conversations, partnerships, and the first Close the Gap community programs.

Close the Gap NextGen

Health education, career exposure, and community leadership.

NextGen is a health equity and healthcare-career program for students interested in understanding how healthcare systems, community conditions, and access to care affect the people they hope to serve.

NextGen Inquiry

Program experience

Students take part in virtual sessions with healthcare professionals and community leaders, explore healthcare and public health careers, and build skills in health equity, advocacy, communication, research, and leadership.

Participants also complete an individual or group project focused on a community health issue and present their work at a final community showcase.

About Close the Gap

Built from a personal understanding of the barriers people face before they reach care.

As a Haitian and a student of medicine and public health, I have seen how limited health education, lack of community support, and difficulty finding trusted resources can affect a person’s health.

I started Close the Gap to better understand those barriers and help connect communities with healthcare organizations, health information, educational opportunities, and resources that can make care easier to navigate.

— Milinda Florvela
