Community

Listen before building.

Our first step is not assuming what communities need. It is learning from the people and organizations already navigating the healthcare system every day.

We are beginning in Miami-Dade with stakeholder listening meetings to understand the concerns local organizations are already noticing — including barriers to healthcare access, gaps in health education, underused resources, and challenges residents face when trying to seek care.

Those insights will help shape future Community Conversations, where residents can share their own experiences and priorities directly.

Close the Gap NextGen

Health. Opportunity. Leadership.

A health equity and healthcare-career experience designed to help students understand the communities they hope to serve — not just the careers they hope to enter.

JANUARY–MARCH 2027 PILOT

A mostly virtual experience with a real community project.

Students will learn from health professionals and community leaders, explore healthcare and public health careers, examine local health inequities, and work individually or in teams on a project that responds to a real community need.

The program concludes with a community health showcase where students present their work to families, partners, healthcare professionals, and community members.

“The gap is often not a lack of care — it is the space between people and the care, information, support, and trust they need to reach it.”

Why Close the Gap

Built from a personal understanding of what disconnection can cost.

Close the Gap was founded by Milinda Florvela, a Haitian medical and public health student who has seen how limited health education, lack of community support, and difficulty finding trusted resources can negatively affect a person’s health.

The goal is simple: listen carefully, understand where gaps still exist, and strengthen the connections between communities, healthcare services, and the resources people need to access care.
