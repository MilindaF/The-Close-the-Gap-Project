Our approach

We start by learning what is getting in the way of care. We then use that information to guide community programs, partnerships, and access to resources.

Current Work

We are starting with organizations that already serve the community.

Before holding broader Community Conversations, Close the Gap is meeting with healthcare organizations, community groups, educators, and local leaders in Miami-Dade. These short meetings help us understand the health concerns and access barriers they are seeing firsthand.

We are also asking about existing services, where residents have difficulty finding or using resources, and where additional support may be useful.

Close the Gap NextGen

Preparing students for careers and leadership in health.

NextGen is a health equity and healthcare-career program for students interested in learning how health systems, community conditions, and access to care affect the people they hope to serve.

STUDENT HEALTH EQUITY PROGRAM

Learn from health professionals. Study local health needs. Build a community project.

Students take part in virtual sessions with healthcare professionals and community leaders. The program introduces healthcare and public health careers, health equity, community health, advocacy, and leadership.

Participants then apply what they learn through an individual or group project focused on a community health issue. Projects are presented at a final community showcase.

About Close the Gap

Created to address the barriers people face before they reach care.

Milinda Florvela founded Close the Gap while training in medicine and public health. As a Haitian, she has seen how limited health education, lack of community support, and difficulty finding trusted resources can make it harder for individuals and families to seek care.

Close the Gap was created to better understand those barriers and build practical connections between communities, healthcare organizations, and the resources available to them.
