Why Close the Gap

Access is more than having services nearby.

People can live surrounded by healthcare resources and still struggle to know where to go, who to trust, what support exists, or how to move forward.

Close the Gap exists to make those pathways clearer by listening to communities, strengthening connections, and helping trusted resources reach the people they are meant to serve.

OUR VISION

We envision healthier, more connected communities where people can navigate care with confidence, trusted resources reach the people they are meant to serve, and organizations work together so access to health and opportunity is not determined by circumstance.

Close the Gap NextGen

Health education, career exposure, and community leadership.

NextGen is a health equity and healthcare-career program for students interested in understanding how healthcare systems, community conditions, and access to care affect the people they hope to serve.

NextGen Inquiry

Program experience

Students take part in virtual sessions with healthcare professionals and community leaders, explore healthcare and public health careers, and build skills in health equity, advocacy, communication, research, and leadership.

Participants also complete an individual or group project focused on a community health issue and present their work at a final community showcase.
